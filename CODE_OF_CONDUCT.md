# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the **MeetoOps** open-source community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and technical opinions.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive code review feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and users of MeetoOps.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information or security credentials without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate corrective action (including warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from project spaces) in response to violations.

*Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.*
